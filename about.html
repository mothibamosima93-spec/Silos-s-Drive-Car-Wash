Built for busy Johannesburg

Silo's Drive is a convenient car wash in Johannesburg offering exterior wash, interior detailing and express services.

Who We Are

With eco-friendly products and less water, without cutting corners on the shine.

Our focus is speed, quality, and customer experience - for busy drivers, Uber and Bolt drivers, and families who just need this one thing off the list.

Why We Started

Existing car washes come with long queues. Some sites are not safe, and some staff are not well trained to handle customer's belongings with care.

Drivers want fast, affordable, reliable cleaning without any risks.

How We Operate

  • Equipment: Pressure washers, a water saving system, vacuums, and canopy over every bay
  • Team on shift: One manager plus six washers, working in shifts to keep queues short from open to close
  • Hours: 7am - 7pm, seven days a week

Less water, same shine

Our water system is built into every bay from day one and eco focus that cuts water usage by 40% without slowing the wash down.

Who We Serve

  • Daily commuters
  • Ride-share drivers
  • Car owners around malls and offices
  • Residential areas across Johannesburg
